# Logic and Sets

Related: [[analysis1-01-logic-sets-functions-numbers-sequences]], [[functions]].

## Propositional logic

Statements, truth values, connectives ($\neg,\wedge,\vee,\Rightarrow,\Leftrightarrow$),
tautologies/contradictions, quantifiers ($\forall,\exists,\exists_1$) and their
negation rules. Proof techniques: direct proof (implication chains), indirect
proof (contraposition, contradiction). See full list of standard tautologies
(De Morgan, modus ponens/tollens/barbara, contraposition, distributive laws) in
[[analysis1-01-logic-sets-functions-numbers-sequences]].

## Sets

Membership, subset/equality, union/intersection/difference, Cartesian product,
power set. Generalized operations over finite families of sets
($\bigcup,\bigcap,\prod$). Ordered tuples and important Cartesian products
$\mathbb R^n$. Intervals $[a,b],(a,b),[a,b),(a,b]$.

## Worked proofs to remember

- $\sqrt2$ is irrational (proof by contradiction via coprimality).
- "$n$ even $\iff n^2$ even" (direct + indirect proof side by side) — a canonical
  example contrasting direct vs. indirect proof technique.
